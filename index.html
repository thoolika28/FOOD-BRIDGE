ABOUT FOOD BRIDGE

Good food should never go to waste.

Food Bridge connects surplus food with people and organisations working to reduce food insecurity.

01 / RESCUE

Rescue Food

Give surplus food another purpose instead of letting it go to waste.

02 / CONNECT

Connect People

Help donors and food recipients discover opportunities to connect.

03 / REDUCE WASTE

Reduce Waste

Encourage responsible food distribution and reduce avoidable waste.
